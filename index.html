Print Preparation & Recommended Stock Guide

layers

Paper Recommendation

We recommend 160–300 GSM Bristol Board (Smooth or Vellum), Hot Press Watercolor Paper (100% cotton), or Heavyweight Matte Presentation Cardstock. Avoid standard 80 GSM copy paper to prevent ink bleed-through.

[Weight: 250 GSM Ideal]
tune

Printer Settings

In your printer dialog, select 'Actual Size' or 100% Scale (do not use 'Fit to Printable Area'). Folio plates are pre-calibrated with 0.5" outer gutter margins for wire-o, spiral, or archival portfolio sleeve binding.

[Scaling: 100% Actual]
radio_button_checked

3-Ring Binder & Margin Guides

Choose between 3-Ring Binder Layout (0.75" inner margin with visual 4.25" / 8.5" drill alignment targets) or Clean Edge / Flush Mount (0.25" uniform borderless frame). Artwork remains uncropped.

[0.75" Safe Punch Gutter]
brush

Coloring Mediums

Tested rigorously for alcohol ink markers (Copic, Ohuhu, Blick Studio), soft-core colored pencils (Prismacolor Premier, Polychromos), and metallic fineliners. Crisp black lines prevent pigment muddying and feathering.

[Solvent Compatible]
